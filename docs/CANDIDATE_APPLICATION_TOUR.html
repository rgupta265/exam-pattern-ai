Candidate Application Tour & Practice Guide

Step-by-step candidate guide for authentic blueprint-verified examination simulations, mobile exam-taking UX, multiple retakes, and custom AI practice drills.

1 Candidate Dashboard
/[locale]/candidate/dashboard

Review your attempted tests, accuracy rate, longitudinal score trends, identified strong/weak topics, and recent examination scorecards.

Quick Action Launch available mock tests or targeted practice drills directly from the dashboard hero cards.
2 Explore Verified Target Exams
/[locale]/candidate/exams

Discover target competitive examinations by category, conducting authority, post, and notification cycle. View verified syllabus topics and blueprint breakdown.

3 Full Examination Mode vs Topic Practice
/[locale]/candidate/mock-tests
  1. Select Full Examination for authentic official simulations.
  2. Inspect Mock ID, exact question count (e.g. 100 Qs), duration (e.g. 120m), marks, and negative marking (-0.25).
  3. Click Start Test or Resume Test.
4 Pre-Exam Instructions & Language Choice
/[locale]/candidate/attempts/[id]/instructions

Read official rules and select your primary question display language (English or Hindi). Check the acknowledgement box and click I am ready to begin.

5 Live Touch Examination Interface
/[locale]/candidate/attempts/[id]

Experience mobile-first touch navigation:

  • Server-Authoritative Timer: Synchronized with server clock; auto-submits on expiration.
  • Interactive Question Palette: Easily jump between sections and marked questions.
  • Real-Time Autosave: Every answer selection is instantly persisted.
  • Refresh Resiliency: Reopening the browser safely resumes your ongoing attempt with exact remaining time.
6 Scorecard, Answers & Time Analysis
/[locale]/candidate/results/[id]

View net marks, percentage, correct/incorrect counts, time spent per question, and step-by-step solution explanations.

7 Retake Support (Same Examination)
Multi-Attempt Support

Retaking an official mock reuses the exact same question set and locked blueprint rules, creating an incremental attempt (Attempt 2, 3...) to measure your improvement over time.

8 Custom AI Practice Drills
/[locale]/candidate/practice/create

Generate targeted dynamic practice drills separate from official mocks using verified syllabus topics or your own notes.

9 Ingesting PDF, DOCX, Images, Text & URLs
Resource Ingestion

Upload reference PDF chapters, Word documents, book photos, paste revision notes, or input safe educational URLs. Gemini AI generates focused MCQs strictly bounded to your source material.
