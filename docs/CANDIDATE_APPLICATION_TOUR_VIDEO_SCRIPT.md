# Candidate Application Tour — Video Walkthrough Storyboard & Script

> **Document Type:** Production Video Storyboard & Narration Script  
> **Platform:** ExamPattern AI Candidate Portal  
> **Target Audience:** Competitive Exam Candidates, Students, Job Aspirants  
> **Approximate Total Duration:** 3 minutes 45 seconds  
> **Voiceover Languages:** English (Primary) / Hindi (Localized Track)

---

## Scene 1: Candidate Portal Overview & Authentication (0:00 - 0:35)

* **Screen:** Candidate Portal Login (`/[locale]/login`) → Candidate Dashboard (`/[locale]/candidate/dashboard`)
* **Visual Action:**
  1. Candidate signs in with registered credentials.
  2. Candidate Dashboard appears showing statistics: Tests Attempted, Accuracy Rate, Longitudinal Progress, and Best Scores.
* **Narration (English):**
  > "Welcome to your ExamPattern AI Candidate Portal. Here, you prepare for high-stakes government and competitive examinations under authentic exam conditions."
* **Narration (Hindi):**
  > "आपके ExamPattern AI अभ्यर्थी पोर्टल में आपका स्वागत है। यहाँ आप वास्तविक परीक्षा परिस्थितियों में सरकारी और प्रतियोगी परीक्षाओं की तैयारी करते हैं।"
* **Expected Result:** Instant access to candidate dashboard and statistics.

---

## Scene 2: Selecting Full Examination Mode & Reading Instructions (0:35 - 1:15)

* **Screen:** `/candidate/mock-tests` → `/candidate/attempts/[id]/instructions`
* **Visual Action:**
  1. Candidate clicks "Mock Tests" on the sidebar.
  2. Selects "Full Examination Mode" on the mode selector.
  3. Clicks "Start Test" on "UP Health Officer 2026 Full Official Mock".
  4. Global Loading Overlay displays "Preparing your examination...".
  5. Pre-exam instructions screen opens: candidate chooses "Bilingual (English + Hindi)" and checks acknowledgement.
* **Narration (English):**
  > "Select Full Examination Mode to take tests strictly calibrated to locked official blueprints—with exact question counts, marks, duration, and negative penalties. Read instructions and choose your preferred language."
* **Narration (Hindi):**
  > "फुल परीक्षा मोड चुनें जो आधिकारिक ब्लूप्रिंट के अनुसार सटीक प्रश्नों, अंकों, समय और नकारात्मक अंकन के साथ तैयार किए गए हैं। निर्देश पढ़ें और भाषा चुनें।"
* **Expected Result:** Instructions acknowledged and live exam environment launched.

---

## Scene 3: Live Touch Exam Interface & Resilient Autosave (1:15 - 2:05)

* **Screen:** `/candidate/attempts/[id]`
* **Visual Action:**
  1. Camera focuses on the server-synchronized countdown timer.
  2. Candidate answers Section A questions: selects Option B.
  3. Uses "Mark for Review & Next".
  4. Opens the collapsible touch question palette to jump between sections.
  5. Demonstrates browser refresh: page reloads instantly without losing answers or elapsed time.
* **Narration (English):**
  > "The live exam interface is optimized for mobile touchscreens. Every answer selection is instantly saved in the background. If your browser closes or refreshes, you resume seamlessly with your exact remaining time."
* **Narration (Hindi):**
  > "लाइव परीक्षा इंटरफ़ेस मोबाइल टचस्क्रीन के लिए पूरी तरह अनुकूलित है। प्रत्येक उत्तर रीयल-टाइम में स्वतः सुरक्षित होता है। ब्राउज़र बंद होने या रीफ्रेश होने पर आप बचे हुए समय के साथ वहीं से शुरू कर सकते हैं।"
* **Expected Result:** Zero data loss, fluid navigation, and server-authoritative timer enforcement.

---

## Scene 4: Exam Submission & Detailed Result Breakdown (2:05 - 2:50)

* **Screen:** Submit Confirmation Modal → Result Scorecard (`/candidate/results/[id]`)
* **Visual Action:**
  1. Candidate clicks "Submit Examination" and confirms in modal.
  2. Scorecard appears: Net Score 86.5/100, Accuracy 91%, Sectional Breakdown.
  3. Candidate inspects Question 4: Candidate Answer vs Correct Answer vs Step-by-Step Detailed Explanation.
* **Narration (English):**
  > "Upon submission, your scorecard is calculated instantly using authoritative negative marking rules. Review your net score, section performance, time spent per question, and comprehensive step-by-step solutions."
* **Narration (Hindi):**
  > "सबमिट करने पर आधिकारिक नियमों के आधार पर आपका स्कोरकार्ड तुरंत तैयार होता है। अपने प्राप्तांक, प्रत्येक प्रश्न पर बिताया गया समय और विस्तृत हल देखें।"
* **Expected Result:** Deep, transparent diagnostic performance analytics.

## Scene 5: Multiple Retakes & Longitudinal Progress (2:50 - 3:15)

* **Screen:** `/candidate/mock-tests` → Attempt History
* **Visual Action:**
  1. Candidate returns to Mock Tests and clicks "Retake Examination".
  2. Mock card updates showing "Attempt 2/10" and "Best Score: 86%".
* **Narration (English):**
  > "Need more practice? Retake the exact same official mock test multiple times. Each retake creates a fresh attempt, allowing you to track your longitudinal score improvements over time."
* **Narration (Hindi):**
  > "अधिक अभ्यास चाहिए? उसी आधिकारिक मॉक टेस्ट को दोबारा हल करें। प्रत्येक रीटेक एक नया प्रयास बनाता है जिससे समय के साथ आपके सुधार का सटीक विश्लेषण होता है।"
* **Expected Result:** Incremental attempt numbering preserving historical transcripts.

---

## Scene 6: Custom AI Practice Drills (3:15 - 3:45)

* **Screen:** `/candidate/practice/create`
* **Visual Action:**
  1. Candidate navigates to "Practice Drills".
  2. Uploads a PDF chapter / enters educational notes.
  3. Generates an instant 20-question topic practice drill.
* **Narration (English):**
  > "In addition to official full mocks, you can create focused AI practice drills from your own study notes, PDFs, or verified syllabus topics. Master every topic at your own pace with ExamPattern AI."
* **Narration (Hindi):**
  > "आधिकारिक मॉक के अलावा आप अपने नोट्स, पीडीएफ या पाठ्यक्रम विषयों से त्वरित एआई प्रैक्टिस ड्रिल भी बना सकते हैं। ExamPattern AI के साथ हर विषय में निपुणता हासिल करें।"
* **Expected Result:** Instant targeted practice drill ready for candidate attempt.
