Administrator Application Tour & Operating Guide

Authoritative walkthrough of the ExamPattern AI console. Understand document verification, blueprint lifecycle state machines, controlled AI generation, and candidate attempt audits.

1 Core Product Principle
AGENTS.md Compliance

ExamPattern AI follows a strict hierarchy where official exam blueprints verified by human administrators serve as the single source of truth. AI is never the final authority for question counts, negative marking rates, or durations.

Authority Rule Once an exam blueprint reaches the LOCKED state, it is permanently immutable. Any official notification corrigendum must spawn a new distinct version.
Expected System Behavior: All mock generation requests strictly reference the locked blueprint snapshot.
2 Admin Authentication & Session Security
/[locale]/login
  1. Navigate to the login portal (/en/login or /hi/login).
  2. Enter your assigned administrator email address and secure password.
  3. Submit the form. Upon authentication, you are redirected to /admin/dashboard.
Security Standard All administrative routes enforce server-side RBAC checks in addition to Next.js middleware protection.
3 Exam & Version Management
/[locale]/admin/exams
  1. Open Exams & Versions menu in the sidebar.
  2. Click Create Exam and enter exam name, code, category, and conducting authority.
  3. Create a distinct Exam Version (e.g., "2026 Regular Notification").
Expected Result: An isolated examination context is initialized ready for official document attachment.
4 Official Documents & Verification
/[locale]/admin/notifications
  1. Upload official advertisement and syllabus PDFs to secure local storage.
  2. Trigger Gemini AI Document Extraction to extract pattern and syllabus nodes.
  3. Perform human verification and click Verify Document.
Extraction Rule Only verified authoritative documents can be used as the baseline for official mock generation.
5 Syllabus Hierarchy & Tree Selector
/[locale]/admin/exams/[id]/syllabus

Configure sections and topics with full tree navigation, "Select All", and parent-child cascades. Every question created or generated maps to a verified syllabus node.

6 Blueprint Configuration & Locking
/[locale]/admin/blueprints

Define total question counts, section question breakdown, marks per item, duration in minutes, and negative marking rates. Once verified, click Lock Blueprint.

State Machine: Blueprint state becomes LOCKED and ACTIVE.
7 Question Bank & Controlled AI Generation
/[locale]/admin/questions

Draft questions manually or generate context-bounded question batches using Gemini AI strictly aligned with verified syllabus topics.

8 Full-Pattern Mock Generation & Validator
/[locale]/admin/mock-tests

Generate official mock examinations matching locked blueprint parameters. The automated Pattern Validation Engine validates question counts, negative marks, duration, and syllabus compliance before publication.

9 Candidate Management & Attempt Audits
/[locale]/admin/candidates

Inspect candidate attempt transcripts (Attempt 1, Attempt 2...), chosen answers, revision history, question time spent, and scorecard evaluations.

10 Audit Logs, Analytics & Settings
/[locale]/admin/audit-logs

Review immutable administrative audit logs capturing user IDs, timestamps, IP addresses, and change diffs.
